Chapter 3

The IPO Trip-Wire Worth Watching

Every prior bull-market top of meaningful size had a giant new-issue moment in the months before it broke.

The bear case the desk takes seriously runs through stock supply. Stocks are a market with supply and demand like any other. When the supply pipe opens up and dumps trillions of new paper onto a market trading at all-time highs, history says that paper struggles to be absorbed and the broader index gets cut hard within months.

1901

US Steel formed

JP Morgan rolls Carnegie and the smaller mills into one company. The new issue alone represented roughly 15% of the total US stock market and ~5% of GDP. The market was cut in half in the months that followed.

1919

RCA listed

The era's marquee technology IPO. Ran hard, then the broader market crashed and RCA gave up most of the move.

1956

Ford goes public

Biggest IPO of its time, ~1% of US GDP at issue. The Ford family owned 85-90% of the stock and used the IPO to diversify, dumping equity onto retail. Stocks crashed shortly after.

2007

Blackstone IPOs

Marquee private-equity insider sale at the top of the cycle. Followed by the GFC into 2008-09.

That is the trip-wire to watch this year. SpaceX is reportedly heading public at a $1.5T to $2T valuation. Anthropic and OpenAI are both queued for what would be the largest tech IPOs in history. The S&P is at all-time highs, retail is engaged, and a window like this is when private holders cash out. Note also the index rules getting bent so that SpaceX can be added on day one, which has the same smell as past late-cycle accommodations. [45:30]

Two of the other classic top ingredients are not yet in place. Funky accounting and circular financing (vendor loans, customer-payment loops, price-to-eyeball ratios from 1999) is showing up at the edges via Nvidia paying its own customers, and that is worth tracking, but it is not yet pervasive. The Fed has also not yet pulled liquidity. If Powell starts shrinking the balance sheet into a market top while the IPO supply hits, that is the third leg. Until that combination arrives, the working read is that the rally has more room.

The crypto angle

The same supply story is the crypto angle. If a $2T SpaceX, plus AI IPOs, sucks liquidity out of equities for a quarter, the spillover effect on BTC is mechanical. Watch for BTC to lead the equity index lower if and when the IPOs print, and to bottom first.

The Bottom Line

  1. Disbelief is the floor. Indices print records while sentiment sits at all-time lows and CTAs got their faces ripped off short. Historically that combination resolves higher. Stay long the index.
  2. The IPO calendar is the trip-wire. SpaceX, Anthropic, and OpenAI all hitting public markets within the same window is the kind of supply event that has preceded every major top going back to 1901. Track when they actually price.
  3. Tech leads, food and large-cap financials lag. Earnings revisions and seasonal flow favor growth into Q3. Avoid $MCD, $SHAK, $DASH and the heavy bank book until the tape proves them otherwise.
