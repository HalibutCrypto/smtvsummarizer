Chapter 3

The Stock Pickers' Market Is Back

JC's chart of the day is the clearest framework on the show.

For most of the bull market, the way to win was to own the index. Individual stock selection didn't matter much because everything moved with everything. JC pulled up a rolling correlation chart (a measure of how tightly individual stocks move together with the broader S&P 500) showing that this relationship has collapsed. The 6-month rolling correlation is at multi-year lows. The 1-year version is "falling off a cliff," in his words. [16:25]

Translation: stocks are doing their own thing again. That's a stock pickers market, and the trader who picks well outperforms the indexer for the first time in a while. Strazza's read on the floor matched the chart before he saw it. "More now than ever," he said. Q1 was a buy-the-index tape. April flipped it.

The frame JC stretched the point with is that "passive investing" is a marketing label. Someone is always making active decisions about what's in your index. When the Dow kicked $XOM out in August 2020 (after 90+ years) and added $CRM, Exxon went on to do +360% while Salesforce became a disaster. $SMCI was added to the S&P in 2024 and is down -75% since. Index committees are stock pickers. They are just bad at it. [14:55]

The crypto angle

Same logic translates one chain over. When crypto correlations break down (alts diverging from $BTC, sectors trading on their own narratives) the "buy the index" approach in CEX/onchain ETFs underperforms picking the right sub-narrative. Today's $DOGE breakout while $BTC sits flat is the crypto-side version of exactly the chart JC pulled up.
