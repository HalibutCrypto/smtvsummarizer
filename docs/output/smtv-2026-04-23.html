Chapter 1

Bitcoin Touched the Scene of the Crime

Same level that capped the 2017 Christmas mania and the 2021 SPAC peak. Now we see if it breaks.

Spot tape was uneventful, but the chart is loud. $BTC sits at ~$78,000 flat, $ETH at $2,330 -2%, $SOL at $86 -1.5%. Total crypto market cap parked at $2.57T. [8:00]

The chart that mattered today was the long-term $BTC chart that JC pulled up. Bitcoin has just retouched the same price level it reached at two prior cycle peaks: the late-2017 Christmas-and-uncle-asking-about-crypto top, and the early-2021 SPAC/ARK/Cathie Wood mania. Both times it rejected hard. Now it's back, and the question on the desk was simple. Is this the bottom, or is this just a bottom that gets retested again? [26:05]

The condition for "this is the move" was confirmation from $ETH and $SOL. Today both faded. So the chart is right at the level but not yet through it. A clean break above the prior peak with alts joining is what would turn this into a real run. A rejection here is the third one at the same level, which would set up another cycle of consolidation.

For positioning today, this is the level you want to size into with stops below. Chasing a breakout from here only makes sense once $ETH and $SOL confirm.

Chapter 2

Tesla's Snoozer Was the Point

The market priced in the future a long time ago. Last quarter's cars don't move the stock anymore.

$TSLA reported and the reaction was meh, which is how this stock has always traded around earnings. Tesla's price-to-earnings ratio (a measure of how expensive a stock is vs. its current profits) sits around 50-60, while the rest of the Mag 7 cluster around 20-30. The stock is priced almost entirely on what the company becomes when Optimus production ramps and robotaxis go national. Current-quarter car deliveries barely move that math.

The car business itself is fine. Deliveries grew year-over-year. The actual interesting numbers were elsewhere. Robotaxi paid miles hit 1.7M last quarter, roughly a 3x. Optimus is set for production later this year. AI capex is going from $11B two years ago, down to $8B last year, up to $25B this cycle. The company is doubling down. [9:20]

Strazza's framework on the chart is that $TSLA went from $80B to $1.3T in market cap in 18 months, the fastest move of that scale in US market history. A multi-year consolidation after that is well-deserved. The level to clear is $385-386. While the rest of big-cap tech has rallied for three weeks, Tesla has hung back. $AMZN is leading the consumer-discretionary group instead, breaking out to ATHs. [13:50]

On the SpaceX IPO that's allegedly coming in June, JC and Spencer were both aligned. "Wouldn't touch it with a 10-foot pole." Stephen Weiss is doing the deep dive on it after the show.

Chapter 3

When the First Move Is the Fake Move

Software hate keeps repeating. Cannabis has faded eight rescheduling headlines and counting.

ServiceNow reported a beat-and-raise quarter last night and the stock dropped -20%. The market keeps freaking out about margin guidance (their forecast for next-quarter profitability) even when the actual results are good. $CRM (Salesforce) and $INTU (Intuit) traded down in sympathy. The pattern in software has been beat-and-raise followed by margin-guidance-driven selloff for several quarters running. [8:55]

The desk's read was that the post-earnings drift eventually reverses. Software is too important to AI infrastructure to sit out forever. $MSFT reports next week into this exact pattern. If it gets margin-punished too, that's the bottom of the trade.

The other fade chapter was cannabis. $MSOS (the US-cannabis ETF, which holds American cannabis operators that can't list on major exchanges due to federal cannabis status) ripped +12% yesterday on a Trump headline that rescheduling is happening "without further delay." Strazza pulled up a long-term chart with circles at every prior rescheduling-headline event going back to 2020.

DEC 2020

House decriminalize bill

House of Representatives passes act to decriminalize cannabis at the federal level. Rallied. Faded.

JUL 2021

Schumer legalization bill

Chuck Schumer introduces bill to legalize cannabis. Rallied. Faded.

OCT 2022 → AUG 2024

Multiple Biden-era reviews

Biden directs AG to review scheduling. AG announces "still working on it" three separate times across two years. Each headline rallied. Each faded.

DEC 2025 → APR 2026

Trump rescheduling executive orders

Executive order in December, DA review headlines in February, reiteration this April. Each rallied. Each has faded so far.

YESTERDAY

"Without further delay"

Latest Trump statement triggers the most recent +12% pop. The 9th rescheduling rally on the same chart.

The pattern says fade. Every cannabis rescheduling rally on this chart has died at some point in the following weeks. Until that streak breaks, this is one to short or sit out. Chasing the pop has lost money eight times running.

The crypto angle

Same pattern translates one chain over. News-headline pops in crypto (a regulatory clarity tweet, an ETF rumor, an exchange listing) almost always fade unless a real technical breakout follows. The actual moves come from clean breaks of well-watched levels (like $BTC through prior cycle highs in Chapter 1), not from headline reactions. If you're sizing into the current setup, weight the chart over the news.
